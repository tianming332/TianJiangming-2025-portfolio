Inside the experiment

Physics
A tetrahedral soft body with XPBD elastic and volume constraints. The rind is firmer; the flesh yields locally. Fixed timesteps, inversion barriers and ground friction keep it together.

Rendering
Native WebGPU renders a rounded 3D surface. A back-depth pass measures optical thickness for absorption and studio refraction. Fresnel reflections, soft shadows, and individually modeled seeds finish the specimen. Refraction and scattering are real-time approximations, not path tracing.

Interaction
Grab and stretch with mouse or touch. Scroll during a grab to gently twist. Seeds and bubbles are embedded in the same tetrahedra as the flesh. Arrow keys nudge, R resets, Space pauses.

MATERIAL STUDIES / RENDERER NOTE

This specimen needs WebGPU.

This soft-body study is authored directly for WebGPU and WGSL. No compatible GPU interface was exposed, so it has deliberately not been replaced by a lesser renderer. Try a current Chrome, Edge, or Safari build on supported hardware.
